We live in dangerous times, and riches are uncertain
things: the way to make them safe, is to bestow
them upon the poor: Eccles. xi. 1, " Cast thy
bread upon the waters: for thou shalt find it after
many days;" if you keep it, you lose; if you cast it
away, you shall find it again.
One had this epitaph upon his tomb:
Habeo quod dedi, perdidi quud servavi: I possess
what I gave; 1 have lost that which I kept.
If you would keep wine or meal, you put them

The above quote is taken from William Greenhill's commentary on the book of the Prophet Ezekiel, on chapter 16:49

Ezek 16:49 Behold, this was the iniquity of thy sister Sodom, pride, fulness of bread, and abundance of idleness was in her and in her daughters, neither did she strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.

So Greenhill is here commenting on this verse about strengthening the hand of the poor.
